📱 Smartphone Basics

Learn simple smartphone features step by step. These skills can make everyday phone use easier.

Basic Smartphone Skills

⚙️ Phone Settings

Learn how to open settings and change basic phone options.

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Choose the required option.
  3. Change the setting carefully.
  4. Go back and check the change.

📷 Camera

Learn how to take a photo using the smartphone camera.

  1. Open the Camera app.
  2. Point the camera at the object.
  3. Tap the camera button.
  4. Open Gallery to see the photo.

📞 Making a Call

Learn how to find a contact and make a phone call.

  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Open Contacts.
  3. Select the person.
  4. Tap the call button.

🌐 Phone Language

Learn how to change the phone language according to your comfort.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Language or System.
  3. Select Languages.
  4. Choose your preferred language.

💡 Important Tip

Change only settings that you understand. If you are unsure, ask a trusted family member or trainer for help.
